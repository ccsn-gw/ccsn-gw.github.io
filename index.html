The bounce signal and what it encodes

Rapid rotation flattens the collapsing core. At bounce it rings, and the peak frequency and amplitude of that burst depend on rotation (T/|W|) and on the stiffness of nuclear matter. That dependence is what our classifiers learn.

Research

What we work on

Core-collapse supernovae

Multidimensional simulations of stellar core collapse, bounce and the post-bounce evolution, including rotation, turbulence and shock dynamics.

Gravitational waves

Gravitational-wave signals from collapsing and rotating stellar cores and proto-neutron stars, and their observation with current and future detectors.

Dense matter and neutrinos

The nuclear equation of state, neutrino radiation transport and the physics of compact objects formed in stellar collapse.

Machine learning

Machine-learning methods for supernova gravitational-wave astronomy: signal classification and inference of source properties.
